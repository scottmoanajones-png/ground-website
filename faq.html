Onboarding is easy. Fill out our contact form or reach out to clients@groundtech.co to obtain an invitation code to our sandbox environment and begin testing. Production access is granted the same way.

Ground offers an API-as-a-service which can be integrated into any platform or product. In addition, you have access to an administrative portal where all of the capabilities of the API are available, plus master account-level management, accounting, and visibility features.

You remain in control of funds at all times. MPC (multi-party computation) controls and approval gates can be layered on for additional security of provisioned blockchain wallets.

Ground's system and smart contracts absorb network fees.

You are invoiced monthly for the usage of the platform. Digital assets in Portfolio Wallets (the provisioned blockchain wallets) are not debited by Ground.

Yield sources are third-party protocols. Ground routes funds according to your configuration but does not manage or guarantee performance of underlying protocols. Any losses in yield source protocols are borne by you. Review the characteristics of each yield source in our documentation before configuring your allocation.

Ground engages third party auditors to review its system architecture, including security access and smart contracts. Smart contract audit by Quantstamp pending.
