Reid Cuming

CEO & Co-founder

Reid Cuming is the CEO & Co-founder of Ground and Co-founder of Superstate. Previously, Reid served as the COO of Superstate, pioneering tokenized securities enabled for DeFi, and as VP & GM of Compound Treasury, the first DeFi company to receive an S&P credit rating.
