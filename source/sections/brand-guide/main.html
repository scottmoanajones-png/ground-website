Brand Guide

A new dimension of money infrastructure.

On-chain yield changed the nature of money. Ground is the company that helps you understand this new dimension and build with it.

Brand core

On-chain yield changed what money can do.

Balances no longer sit still. They can connect to a live yield grid with routing, timing, and control. Ground gives that system a clear operating model. The brand should express that shift with calm, technical confidence.

Credible

Every decision should feel supportable, serious, and ready for institutional scrutiny.

Elegant

Clarity should carry the work. The system should feel refined without becoming decorative.

Assured

Ground should sound and look like a company that already understands the field it operates in.

Logo

The mark is the reference point in the field.

Everything we make is built on the grid. The mark is the fixed point inside it: a grounding layer that helps the rest of the system read clearly and hold together.

Primary lockup

Use black on light surfaces and white on dark surfaces. The mark should feel easy to find and impossible to confuse with ornament.

Type

Typography should feel measured, confident, and easy to read.

Ground uses IBM Plex only: Serif for display, Sans for reading and support, Mono for labels and operational scaffolding. The website is a reading surface first, so type should carry more of the work than visual noise.

Display

Embedded yield.
Built to be read.

IBM Plex Serif 400. Use for Hero, Section, Title S, and Title M roles. It should feel editorial, but never ornamental.

Reading

Any stablecoin or cash balance on your platform already carries potential yield. Ground connects it to the full on-chain yield grid with the confidence of serious infrastructure.

Reading Base is 21px. UI Base is 16px. This lets the site feel generous and readable without making utility text feel swollen.

Grid + Motion

The grid is the field where signal moves.

The grid represents the infrastructure beneath every balance. Top-down shows structure and control. Isometric shows route, depth, and yield. Together they explain how value moves through the field.

Perspective

Top-down and isometric are the two core camera states.

The shift between them is the signature move: the same infrastructure, shown with more depth and more usable information.

Signal

Height, routing, and active cells should encode real system behavior.

Use block height for value or activity, and the signal range for live routes, wake states, and moments of activation.

Foundation

The field should feel infinite, exact, and already there before anything activates.

Motion is not there to decorate the page. It is there to show that the infrastructure can carry signal to the right place.

Voice

Sound credible, elegant, and assured.

The tone should be declarative, measured, and clear. Technical without becoming cold. Ambitious without sounding speculative.

Credible

Say the thing cleanly. Let the confidence come from clarity, not volume.

Elegant

Prefer clean structure, calm language, and restraint over flourish.

Assured

Sound like a serious partner with range, not a product trying to prove it belongs.

Exact

Prefer precise claims, concrete operating language, and real system behavior over abstraction.

Avoid hype verbs, consumer-fintech sparkle, and language that feels louder than the system itself.
